# IEC 60947-2:2024 ⟷ GB/T 14048.2—2020 Annexes (`Annexes A–R`)

> **Conditional Disclosure**: Load only for special derivatives (`CBR`, `MRCD`, `CBI`, `ICB`, `PV DC`, `CBAR`), `IT` systems, aluminium conductors, power loss, EMC, or coordination curves. Full edition divergences & print errata: see [edition_divergences_and_errata.md](edition_divergences_and_errata.md).
